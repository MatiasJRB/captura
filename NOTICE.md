# Origins and third-party components

The Android recorder and Drive worker were extracted from an existing MIT-licensed
personal automation repository, not its agents, memory or data. The original MIT
notice for praveen-ks-2001 is retained conservatively in LICENSE; the selected recorder
and worker first appeared in source commits c18fd63 and f12d490 and were subsequently
modified for this extraction. New contributions are under the same MIT terms.
No private brand assets, recordings, transcripts, signing keys or source history copied.

Bundled Gradle wrapper scripts/JAR: Gradle, Apache-2.0; their embedded notices remain.
Wrapper distribution version and checksum are pinned in android/gradle/wrapper.
Full Gradle license is retained in `licenses/Gradle-LICENSE.txt`.

Downloaded at build time (not relicensed by this repository): Android Gradle Plugin,
Google Play Services auth (Google terms, not a FLOSS-only Android build), JUnit and
org.json. Release APK distribution requires reviewing the resolved dependency/license
notices. This app is not currently eligible for the main F-Droid repository unchanged.

External, not bundled: rclone (MIT), whisper.cpp (MIT), ffmpeg (license depends on its
build), Whisper and Silero VAD model files (review the exact model license before
redistribution). No binaries or models are shipped here except the Gradle wrapper.

The viewer's example prose is newly authored fictional content; no human recording
or synthetic voice generated by a third-party service is distributed as demo data.

This document is a provenance inventory, not a warranty or a completed legal audit.

Optional local voice implementation resolves Vosk Android 0.3.75 (Apache-2.0,
https://github.com/alphacep/vosk-api) and JNA 5.18.1 (upstream dual Apache-2.0 /
LGPL-2.1-or-later, https://github.com/java-native-access/jna). These dependencies
are downloaded at build time, not vendored here. Keep their upstream notices when
distributing binaries. Spanish Vosk model small-es-0.42 is optional, supplied outside
Git, and listed upstream as Apache-2.0: https://alphacephei.com/vosk/models.
