## Setup help

To help someone install or configure Captura (the iPhone app, the Mac worker, Google
Drive, or errors from `ios/scripts/check.py`, `configure.py`, `capture drive-setup` or
`capture doctor`), use the `captura-setup` skill: `.claude/skills/captura-setup/SKILL.md`.
The guides it drives
are `docs/ios.md` (Spanish summary: `docs/ios.es.md`) and `docs/worker.md`.
