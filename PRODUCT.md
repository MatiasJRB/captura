# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Offline static HTML reader generated by a Python stdlib CLI; native Android (Java) and
iOS (SwiftUI) recorders and a local Python worker remain separate tools. No web framework/backend required.
## Users
People who want to reuse an audio-to-agent idea in their own technical setup.
## Product Purpose
Share a minimal open-source contribution, not validate a commercial product.
Turn manually captured phone audio into local reviewable text for an external agent.
## Operating Context
Android or iPhone offline capture, optional Drive, macOS local worker, original-preserving review.
## Capabilities and Constraints
No embedded secretary or personal memory; no execution of spoken commands; no
speaker identity guarantee. Own (or organization-provided) OAuth, signing and model setup. Demo text is fictional.
## Brand Commitments
Working name Captura, not yet cleared or final. Neutral assets; no private studio brand.
## Product Principles
Visible consent; originals preserved; untrusted text; external agents; small scope.
